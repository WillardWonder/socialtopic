How this relates to the patent

The patent describes a system that infers a person’s current focus, finds matching topic nodes in a changing hierarchy, points those nodes to relevant destinations, and updates matching based on preferences and behavior. SignalScope translates that mechanism into public-data research: the topic you enter is the current focus; recurring ideas become topic nodes; actual public records and pages become destinations; your weighting and feedback change ranking; repeated runs show which nodes are emerging or fading.

What is implemented vs. modeled

Implemented: live multi-source search, topic extraction, ranking, source-family support, feedback-based reranking, and repeated-run node comparison. Modeled: “emerging/fading” is based on changes between your local snapshots, not a globally validated trend model. Not implemented: private profiling, biometric inference, or person-level voter-file enrichment.
